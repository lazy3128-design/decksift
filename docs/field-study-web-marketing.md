# Field Study: Competitive Video Research For A Web Marketing Team

This anonymized field study paraphrases feedback from one participant with permission to publish it. The participant reported using DeckSift as part of a 10-person web marketing team's research workflow.

## Participant And Use Case

The team uses DeckSift when analyzing video structure and researching competitor advertising videos, including videos found through sources such as the Meta Ad Library and YouTube advertising. Users remain responsible for following source-platform terms and ensuring they have permission to obtain and analyze the material.

Before using DeckSift, the participant could create transcripts and analyze or summarize spoken content, but found it difficult to review how the video itself was produced: slide design, presenter framing, captions, and the sequence in which those visual elements appeared.

## Reported Value

The participant reported that DeckSift made it easier for the team to inspect the visual construction of campaign videos frame by frame. Pairing extracted frames with existing transcripts helped the team trace both the message and its presentation, use competitor research as a reference, and improve the quality of planning for original in-house videos.

The value was not transcription itself. It was preserving visual evidence that transcription normally loses:

- which design appeared at a given point
- how the presenter and captions were composed
- how visual presentation changed as the message progressed
- which frames could support a research finding or production decision

## Improvement Requests

The participant's main request was faster processing and export. The participant also requested an installable web-app experience.

Subsequent releases improved extraction quality and review ergonomics, and the participant reported being broadly satisfied with the current workflow. Processing-speed measurement and optimization remain future work. PWA packaging is tracked on the project roadmap.

## Limitations Of This Evidence

- This is one participant's qualitative report about use within a 10-person team, not independently verified usage analytics or a broad adoption claim.
- The feedback is paraphrased and anonymized rather than presented as a direct quotation.
- The participant approved anonymous publication of this summary.
- No controlled measurement of time saved or production-quality improvement was conducted.
- The videos and extracted frames used in the participant's work are not published.
- Users are responsible for ensuring they have the rights or permission required to obtain and analyze source material.

## Follow-Up

Future field studies should record video duration, selected detection mode, processing time, output count, review time, and specific false positives. This will make performance and workflow improvements measurable across releases.
